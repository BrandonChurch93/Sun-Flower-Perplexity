## What was measured against the critics' words

- The measures. M3 reads 0.828 at `final` where round 16 read 0.797: the youngest leaves under the bud, the bud's crown and the lens at f/11 lie between the two cuts. No grain and no sharpening was added to move it.
- The background that changes (R4 severe: "A range of distant hills appears on the left at f0627 to f0654, is gone by f0709, and comes back on the right at f0872"). Seen by the Builder on the critic's sheet `set-D-01.png`: a pale far ridge stands left of the plant at 20.9 and 21.8 s, is gone from 22.7 s, and stands on the right at 29.1 and 30.0 s. Those times fit the ramp the last fix pass gave the night's hills (`6e1fbbc`: faint at 20.0 s, dark by 22.0 s, paling from 28.0 to 30.0 s), put in so that no line changed height at the two cuts. The cause is not proved by a render, but it is the likely one: a tone of the world that moves inside the unit, where only the cut may change the world. The rows that fill in from 20.0 to 22.7 s are the neighbors growing and the tops that rise over the horizon at the left from 28.2 s are their buds; both are right in kind, and their pace was not looked at on consecutive frames.
- The one frame blobs and the hair that comes and goes (R4 major twice). Seen by the Builder on `set-D-03.png`: a soft round blob left of the tip in frame 750 only, and a thin pale upright stroke over the right leaf in frames 753, 754 and 757. Each is something that crosses close to the lens within one frame; which part draws it (a mote of the night's air, a falling bead) is not found, nothing was switched off to look.
- The droplets (R3 major: "spherical water droplets hang from the upper-left leaf tip, middle-right leaf edge, and serrated teeth ... despite this part of the film having no water, frost, or dew"). The approved `night` image shows a drop hanging from a leaf tip and wet glints along the blades (tile a of region 2 in `set-A-02.png`), round 16's match critic asked for exactly those ("leaves lack surface veining, wet sheen, and edge dew droplets (including the hanging water drop in region 5)"), and `kit/02-SUBJECT.md` sets dew beginning to form at night. The surface critic is not shown the approved image.
- The neighbors (R1 severe, R3 severe, two jurors). At f/11 the single plants behind the hero are soft shapes. R3 reads them as "flat, un-serrated triangular cards in a single uniform green on straight stick stems with geometric star buds", R1 misses the approved's "group of smaller distinct buds" and finds the field "darker and more crowded". `1c5400b` darkened the rows (a third of the moon and of the sky) because round 16 found the crop too light. Both critics ask for the same thing: a few whole plants with their own leaves and buds at different depths, with a restrained cool rim.
- The plant (R1 severe twice). The kit frames the night closer than its image on purpose (`kit/03-METHOD.md`, section 7), so the canopy reads wider. The fix passes moved the crown (the youngest leaves ride close under the bud, `9880078`) and the bud's bracts (`2d0362b`, `83f7e0e`). The broad cupped leaf across the stem at the approved's height is still missing, and `ea51851` (blades no longer turned flat to the lens) stayed out because it made the blades narrower still.

One issue is declined (R3 major, the droplets): it asks the film to move away from the approved `night` image, which shows them. It stands under "Declined issues" in `kit/PROGRESS.md`. Nothing else is declined.
