Settled, for every file:
- The film is made to a piece of music that is not attached. Its parts change on the bars of that music.
- It is one continuous view of one plant in one place, from seed to flower. While the plant grows, the sky runs through days and nights within seconds, as in time lapse photography.
- Twice the time lapse stops and the plant does not grow: once under frost and once in a rain storm. In the frost part there are hard cuts to close views of the frosted plant and back.
- As the flower opens the camera moves in close on the head, then draws back to show the whole field.
