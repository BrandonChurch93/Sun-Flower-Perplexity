# Round 27 · Version 2 · the record round

Judged commit `d42a869` (engine `9c62c3b3b10c7180`), the frozen build of version 2. The film was drawn once from it, 3,810 frames at 1920 x 1080 and 30 frames a second, at the draft tier (one sample a frame, the look of live play), and joined into one file; frames 60, 1055 and 3523 taken out of the joined file match the lossless frames in the packs, so the round judges this film. Every critic and every juror is a fresh agent that received its files as attachments and nothing else, under a neutral name (`reviewer_509` to `reviewer_635`); which file is which frame is in `KEY.json`, what each one was sent is in the `MANIFEST` files, and the jurors' answers are word for word in `R2-JURORS.md`.

Families: R1 and R3 rotate through GPT, Gemini and Claude (R1-1 GPT, R1-2 Gemini, R1-3 Claude, R1-4 GPT, R1-5 Gemini, R1-6 Claude; R3-1 Claude, R3-2 GPT, R3-3 Gemini, R3-4 Claude, R3-5 GPT); R4-1 Gemini, R4-2 Claude; R5 Grok; R6 GLM; R7 GPT. A pack over ten files goes to more than one critic, each with its own part. The jury: fourteen judged frames and the eight new target pictures, five jurors each, one from each of five families (GPT, Gemini, Claude, GLM, Grok), 110 jurors; the votes on the five approved pictures of the first film are those of rounds 3, 7 and 9, as the kit orders.

One seat was asked twice. The critic of R4-1 (`reviewer_520`, asked 19:52 UTC) had not answered after 16 minutes, so a replacement of the same family (`reviewer_635`) was sent the same pack at 20:08, with the rule written down before either answered: if both answer, the lower score counts. Both answered, 4 at 20:12 and 5 at 20:27. Both cards are kept (`R4-1.json`, `R4-1b.json`) and R4-1 counts as 4.

What the critics were told is meant, and so not a fault: the film is one continuous view of one plant in one place; it has four hard cuts, all in the frost, each on a downbeat; the plant does not grow in the frost and in the storm; the rest is meant to look like time lapse photography, with days and nights passing in seconds; the light steps up on one frame when the storm tears open (69.43 s). For the pairs against the target pictures, eight differences were named as meant (logged in `kit/v2/PROGRESS.md`, 18:15 UTC).
