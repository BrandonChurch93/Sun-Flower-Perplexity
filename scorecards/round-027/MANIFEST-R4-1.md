Settled, for every file:
- The film is one continuous view of one plant in one place. It has four hard cuts, all in the frost part: to close views of the frosted plant and back, each on a downbeat of the music. The cuts are meant.
- In the frost part and in the rain part the plant does not grow. That is meant.
- The other parts are meant to look like time lapse photography: days and nights pass within seconds, the clouds streak, and the leaves take a slightly different pose on every frame. Judge how well that is done, not that it is done.
- At 69.43 s the storm ends on one frame: the light steps up as the cloud tears open. That step is meant.
