## What was measured against the critics' words

- The soil and the middle ground (R1 severe at 1.5 s and at 6.5 s: "dark soil clods only form a narrow mound around the stem while the left and right sides drop away into smooth blur", "a smooth, featureless tan-brown blur"; three jurors name the soil). The fix passes gave the ground lumps, crumbs and tufts near the stem; further out the ground is the simpler kind and the lens turns it into one even tone.
- The far skyline (R1 severe: "a pale, washed-out smooth gray silhouette instead of a dark, high-contrast deep blue-teal tree-lined ridge"). Known before the round: the far trees read 87,93,94 against the approved's 25,49,54 at 6.5 s (round 14's notes), an open item since the last fix pass, when a third mist was rolled over the field to take away a band that read as water (`dd145bf`). The mist that hides the band also lifts the ridge.
- The seed leaves at 6.5 s (R1 severe, both surface critics, two jurors: "bright white polka-dot droplets scattered over their dark surfaces", "tiny flat white specks, all about the same size and evenly scattered", "faint, evenly spaced parallel lines run across the leaf like a tiled texture"). Seen by the Builder in the pack before any critic had it: even pale dots on both seed leaves where the approved has clean leaves with a few beads at the rim. The dots are the shared dew layer at one size. The lines across the left leaf were not seen before this round and are not traced yet.
- The hook and the hull at 1.5 and 2.0 s (R1 severe, R3 major: "the seed shell looks dry and matte with a uniform bright-green side slit and no wet soil crumbs", "the stem skin is a single smooth, even waxy surface with a perfectly clean green-to-red gradient", "the dark and light stripes are evenly spaced and of even width like a printed texture"). The crack lines that spread from the stem and the crumbs that tip and roll, which `kit/03-METHOD.md` asks for, are still missing.
