## What was measured against the critics' words

- The camera (R4 major: "The stem drifts left in frame from 30 s to about 37 s, then moves right again by 38 to 40 s. Between 37.2 s and 38.2 s the plant also suddenly gets larger in frame"). Read from the camera's path by the Builder (`out/work/scratch/campath.ts`, frames 900 to 1200): the plant's place across the frame falls from 0.566 at 30.0 s to 0.529 at 36.0 s and rises to 0.579 at 40.0 s, on its way to the bloom's 0.648 at 45.0 s. The turn is in the fitted keys: the approved `frost` image has the plant at 0.535 of the width, the approved `bloom-opening` at 0.648. The distance does not jump (0.731 m at 37.0 s, 0.734 m at 38.0 s; the focal length 36.7 and 36.4 mm). What grows in that second is the plant, the bud and the neck under it, in front of a camera that holds its distance from 36 to 40 s. Both go to Phase 5's smoothing pass.
- The leaves between 37.2 and 38.2 s (R4 major: "none of the earlier leaves can be followed into the new ones"). Not followed on consecutive frames by the Builder. On the path the aim rises with the tip (1.269 to 1.319 m) while the horizon falls 21 px in that second, so lower leaves leave the frame at its foot and younger ones come in under the bud. Whether each leaf keeps one path through that second is still to be looked at on consecutive frames at native size.
- The background from 38.2 s (R4 major: "The dense, low, leafy frosted field becomes sparse tall stalks with heads, a hard distant hill line appears, and the fog thins sharply"). Seen by the Builder on the critic's sheet `set-D-01.png`: the hill line shows from 38.2 s and the rows stand as stalks with heads at 39.1 and 40.0 s. Known parts of it: the fog sheet between the plant and the first row is gone by 39 s (`577063f`), the neighbors lead the plant into bloom so their buds stand on bare necks first, and the frost on the upper leaves gives way toward the end as `kit/02-SUBJECT.md` asks. The fog thinning far enough to show the hills is the world changing inside the unit: open.
- The light on the plant (R1 severe: "it does not produce the target's luminous warm rim on the frost, hairs and droplets"). The last fix pass made the halo tighter and amber (`a5484c4`, `ab73383`); the rim on the plant was not raised. By `harness_builder`'s boxes on the line's head the near rows' darks are 12 to 15 levels lighter than the approved's and the right middle's median is 10 low.
- The plant's shape and leaves (R1 severe, R3 major). Known before the round (decision of 23:19 UTC): at 1080 the leaves are thin with little rime where the approved has broad rimed ones, and `ea51851`, which stayed out, would have made them thinner. The measures are green on a sharp zone of 123069 pixels.
- No light through the leaves (R3 major: "backlit leaf blades and thin bract margins remain completely opaque dark slate-green"). In the frost the plant's skin carries a pale bloom and the sun is a disc in fog; the approved image shows its leaves dark against the fog with white rims, so how much glow is right here is read from the approved image and not from the critic's wish alone.

Nothing is declined.
