# Cues

Timings for sound. The film is 60 seconds at 30 frames a second, 1800 frames, frame 0 to frame 1799. Timecodes are minutes:seconds:frames at 30 frames a second. At 120 beats per minute a beat is 15 frames and a bar of four beats is 2 seconds, so bar 1 starts at 00:00:00 and every cut lands on a bar line.

## Cuts

Four hard cuts. The frame before each is entirely the old world and the frame on it is entirely the new one. The plant and the camera carry straight across.

| Cut | Timecode | Seconds | Frame | Bar |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `dawn` to `rain` | 00:08:00 | 8.0 | 240 | 5 |
| `rain` to `night` | 00:20:00 | 20.0 | 600 | 11 |
| `night` to `frost` | 00:30:00 | 30.0 | 900 | 16 |
| `frost` to `bloom` | 00:40:00 | 40.0 | 1200 | 21 |

There is no cut at 00:52:00 (frame 1560, bar 27). `bloom` and `field` are one golden evening and one continuous shot; the frame is only where the `bloom` clip ends and the `field` clip starts.

## Music

- The cuts are the beats. Each lands on the first beat of a bar: bars 5, 11, 16 and 21.
- Start small. `dawn` is close and quiet: one sound for the soil, room for the husk at 3.6 to 4.2 s.
- `rain` adds weight from bar 5; leave a hole for the thunder at 17.2 s, just before bar 10 ends.
- `night` (bar 11) is the still point: thin the arrangement, keep the pulse.
- `frost` (bar 16) starts the build toward the bloom.
- The bloom is the peak. Build through bars 21 and 22, let the first petal at 44.1 s open the top of the arrangement, and land the peak on the held face at 50.5 s (the middle of bar 26).
- The field is the release. The camera starts to leave at 50.5 s and from bar 27 (52.0 s) the crane lifts: let the music open out and fall away under it to the last frame at 01:00:00. There is no fade out in the picture; the last frame is a finished picture, so the sound may ring past it in the edit.
