Remove the out-of-focus green leaves and stems in the lower left corner of the frame completely. - Checks passed: the take was scaled to the size of the approved image and compared with it region by region (left, right and top strips, the best shift searched within 20 px): best shift 2 px down in the right strip before the script moved it up by 2 px; the left strip is where the companion's leaves were painted out, so it cannot be compared. It was looked at in native-size crops of its four corners and of the place where the plant stood: no text, no watermark, no object that was not there before, no leftover of the plant.
