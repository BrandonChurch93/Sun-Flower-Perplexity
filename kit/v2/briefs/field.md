# Brief: the field

Your folder is `src/field/`. Your branch is `agent/field`, your looks name is `field`.

The hero plant stands 1.5 m ahead of the first row of a field of sunflowers of its own age. `film.ts` builds `Field`, calls `field.prepare(tau, tauClose, inputs, inputsClose, heightM)` and `field.look(pose, tanX, aspect)` once a frame, and shows it: keep those calls. Beside the hero stands one companion plant (`src/director/companion.ts`, `companionPlace` in `src/field/layout.ts`), which `film.ts` draws.

In version 1 the field was the weakest thing and its last shot failed the jury (1 of 5 took it for footage; four said "web 3D demo"). The critics: "hundreds of field sunflowers face flat toward the camera like uniform yellow-and-black bullseyes and remain pin-sharp to the back of the field"; "the flowers stop short of the horizon leaving a wide featureless dark band, the layered blue-grey hills and textured wooded treeline are replaced by a flat dark" band; "the field stops on a hard line and gives way to a wide, flat, dark band with no rows, plants, texture or perspective"; "foliage shadows are lifted to a washed-out olive-green with a dense bush-like wall of uniform bright-green leaves along the front of the field"; "neighbor plants have flat horizontal cap-like tops and smooth featureless dark leaves with no rim highlights"; in the rain, "the dense field of out-of-focus young seedlings across wet furrows is replaced by a flat black band with only a few sparse, blurry plants".

## What changes

1. One field for the whole film. Read "What version 1 left behind" in `COMMON.md`. `src/field/layout.ts`, `far.ts` and `index.ts` read version 1's scene times (`rainAt`, `nightAt`, `NIGHT_LOW`, `LAST_WORLD_FROM_S`, `segmentStart`, a field that fell behind in the rain scene and jumped in age at a cut). The neighbours now simply grow with the hero on `tau`, each a little ahead or behind, each its own kind, and their weather comes from the dials and from the same `PlantInputs` the hero gets. In a hold they stand still, sway and take the rain or the frost exactly as the hero does (`stateUniforms`). Nothing pops.
2. It is seen all film long, not just at the end. From bar 5 on rows of seedlings stand behind the hero, out of focus (`references/approved/dawn-seedling.png`); in the frost hold they are small frosted seedlings in rows fading into mist (`references/v2/keys/frost.png`); in the storm hold, seen from 2.45 to 2.10 m and only 0.27 to 0.30 m above the soil with more depth of field, they are a dense stand of young plants across wet furrows, thrown about by the gusts (`references/v2/keys/storm.png`); in the climb they rise with the hero and close the horizon (`references/v2/keys/climb.png`, `late.png`). Rows must read as rows: the field is planted 0.75 m between rows and 0.45 m between plants (`config.field`).
3. The crane (bars 65 to 73, film seconds 110.6 to 127, `references/approved/field.png`, `references/v2/keys/last.png`). The camera rises from head height to about 4 m and looks over the whole field into the setting sun (azimuth 225, 3 degrees up falling to the horizon) at 24 mm with deep focus. What the references show and version 1 did not: heads that face mostly one way but each a little differently, many seen from behind or from the side as dark discs with a glowing fringe of backlit rays, since the camera looks into the sun and open heads face east, away from it; plants of uneven height, some heads nodding, some still in bud; leaves as dark masses with bright rims and a few glowing through; the rows drawing together toward the horizon, getting finer and paler and dissolving into the haze under the sun with no line where the field ends; long shadows lying toward the camera across the ground in front. The far land beyond about 66 m is the place agent's (`src/place/`), on the branch `agent/place`: tell `/root` where your field ends in every direction so the two can be joined, and look at its pictures on `agent/place-looks`.
4. Wind. A gust is seen crossing a field as a wave of bending heads and turning leaves. `dials.wind` is 0.48 at the bloom. One wave every few seconds, on `uReal`, travelling toward azimuth 42.
5. Cost. The field is most of the film's triangles. Say in your report how many draw calls and instances it uses at the storm hold and at the last frame, at `view.quality` `film`, `high` and `low`, and make `low` cost about half of `high` without a visible hole.

## What is judged

1. `npm run bench -- --sky sky-0 --only all --view film --format 16x9 --tier review --shots 07,10,13,14,15,16` and `--view crane --shots 15,16`, beside the references.
2. Clips with `--only all --view film`: `--clip 110,127` at the preview size (the whole crane), `--clip 43.5,50.5` (two days of growth), `--clip 57,61` (the storm's gusts).
3. `--count` prints PASS, and the line of `COMMON.md` prints nothing for your folder.
