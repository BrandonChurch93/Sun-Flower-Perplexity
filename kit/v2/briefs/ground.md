# Brief: the ground

Your folder is `src/ground/`, without `src/ground/puddles/` (the rain agent has it). Your branch is `agent/ground`, your looks name is `ground`.

The soil is in more frames than the plant is: it is all there is in frame 0, it fills the lower half of the frost hold, and it is the stage of the storm. Version 1's soil is good and stays (`src/ground/index.ts` is the class `Ground`, which `film.ts` loads, shows and calls with `setState(def.ground, cover)` every frame: keep those calls as they are). Your work is to make it one piece of ground over two minutes and fifteen days, and to raise the three moments below to the references.

## What changes

1. Drive it by the plant's clock and the dials only. Read "What version 1 left behind" in `COMMON.md`. `Ground` and its shaders lean on version 1's scene times in many places (`keptS`, `fieldS`, `rainS`, `nightS`, `uRainS`, `uKeptS`, `uField`, `furrow.fromS`, `grassTimes`, the `settled` logic in `setState`). Each of those becomes a function of `tau` (growth: grass, the furrow the water will stand in, the clods weathering down as weeks pass) or of a dial (`wet`, `puddle`, `frost`, `dew`, `cold`, `rain`, `burst`). The sets that were swapped at a cut (`when`, `young`, `from`, `close` in `index.ts`) must change without a pop: over time, by a dissolve that cannot be seen as one, or while they are out of frame.
2. Light from anywhere. `uSunWay`, `fieldLight` and whatever else assumes a sun direction have to work for a sun that rises at azimuth 175 to 187, crosses at 60 degrees high and sets at 198 to 226, fifteen times, and for the moon at azimuth 168, 13 degrees up. Low sun must rake the clods with long shadows and warm rims; noon must be flat and bleached; night must be near black with a cold sheen.
3. The time lapse. From bar 5 a day passes in 6.9 seconds and from bar 25 in 3.4. Shadows of clods sweep round like clock hands: they must be smooth, not stepping or crawling. Version 1's critic on the first frames: "soil clods far from the emerging hypocotyl hook continuously morph, swell, and crawl in shape every frame ... reading as an artifact". Nothing in the soil may move unless something moves it: the hook lifting it, rain hitting it, water filling it. Grass grows on `uTime` and sways on `uReal`.
4. Where it ends. Find how far your soil reaches and tell `/root` in your first heartbeat: the place agent builds the land beyond it and the two must join without a line. The fog (`uFog...`) already takes the far soil toward the sky's colour.

## The three moments

- Frame 0 and the first two seconds (`references/v2/keys/open.png`, `references/approved/dawn-sprout.png`): blue hour, then the first sun. The camera is 2.3 cm above the soil, 15 cm from where the seed lies, at 60 mm: every crumb is a boulder. Dark, damp, crumbly soil with dew beads catching the sky, a few grass blades, and it must read as a photograph in its first frame. Version 1's jury failed frame 0 ("web 3D demo") and its motion critic wrote: "the hook does not push up out of the soil; it grows as a whole from a small arch into a big one". The soil has to break: a crack runs, crumbs lift and tip and roll off as the arch comes up, and they stay where they fell. The plant agent owns the hook itself; the emergence numbers you share are in `src/ground/soil.ts` (`emergence`, `hole`, `crack`), which the plant reads. If you change them, tell `/root` and the plant agent will be told.
- The frost hold, bars 17 to 25, 28.3 to 42 s (`references/v2/keys/frost.png`, `references/approved/frost.png`): the camera sits 7.5 cm above the soil, 58 cm from the plant, level, at about 48 mm, for almost 14 seconds of real time. Hoar frost on every clod: white crystals on the upper edges and in the air side, dark soil in the hollows, sparkle that turns on and off as the camera drifts, never a white coat of paint. From bar 23 the sun clears the mist and the frost melts from the sunlit sides first (`frost` falls, `dew` and `wet` rise): the soil darkens where it melts and beads stand on it.
- The storm hold, bars 33 to 41, 55.7 to 69.5 s (`references/v2/keys/storm.png`, `storm-flash.png`, `break.png`, `references/approved/rain.png`): the camera is 2.45 m back creeping in to 2.10 m, 0.27 to 0.30 m high, tilted up about 4 degrees, at 38 mm, so the soil between about 1.3 m in front of the lens and the plant fills the lower sixth of the frame at a grazing angle. Wet black soil with coarse clods there, not grass tufts, standing water in the furrow beside the plant (the rain agent draws the water in `src/ground/puddles/`; you give it the basin, by `Ground.glsl` and `rippleBox` as now), a wet sheen that picks up the sky, and at the flash (`now.dials.flash`) every wet facet answers. Version 1's critic: "the ground is dull, matte grey-black mud with smooth blobs and added stiff green grass tufts, missing the coarse clod detail and sparkling wet specular highlights". At bar 41 the sun breaks through from azimuth 201, 11 degrees up, into the lens a little left of the plant: the wet soil must blaze with it.

## What is judged

1. `npm run bench -- --sky sky-0 --only sky,ground --view film --format 16x9 --tier review --shots 01,02,07,10,11,12` beside the references named above.
2. Clips with `--only sky,ground --view film`: `--clip 0,4` (the soil breaks), `--clip 43.5,50.5` (two days of shadows), `--clip 38,43` (the thaw).
3. `--count` prints PASS. Your report gives draw calls and texture megabytes, and what `view.quality` `low` saves.
4. The line that must print nothing (see `COMMON.md`).

A first heartbeat with pictures within 30 minutes. Your report goes to `/root` 150 minutes after you start, or sooner if you are done.
